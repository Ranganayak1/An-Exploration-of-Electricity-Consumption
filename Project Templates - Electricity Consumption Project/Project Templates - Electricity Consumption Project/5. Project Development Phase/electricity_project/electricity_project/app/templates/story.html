The Story of India's Electricity Demand, 2019–2020

Three scenes walking through the pandemic's impact on national power consumption.

Scene 1 of 3

Change in Overall Consumption Trends

Through most of 2019 and early 2020, India's electricity demand followed a familiar seasonal rhythm — rising through the summer cooling season, easing in winter. Then in March 2020, a nationwide lockdown was announced. By April, consumption had fallen sharply below the same month a year earlier, before beginning a phased recovery as restrictions eased.

Scene 2 of 3

Regional Variations in Demand

The impact was not uniform. Industrially dense Western and Northern states — home to large manufacturing bases — saw sharper drops when factories shut. Regions with a larger share of residential and agricultural load felt a softer hit, as household consumption held up or even increased during the lockdown.

Scene 3 of 3

Recovery After Lockdown

From June onward, as India progressively "unlocked," electricity demand began climbing back. By the final quarter of 2020, several states had matched or exceeded their pre-COVID baseline — a sign of economic activity resuming — while others lagged, still recovering as the year closed.
